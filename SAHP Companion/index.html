list_alt Standard Operating Arrest Workflow
Step 1: Handcuff Suspect (Flex 'Z' Muscle)
Sir/Mam I am going to be putting you in handcuffs now, please stand still.
*Always holster weapon before action. Suspect must stand still. If fleeing, taze suspect.
Step 2: Grab by Arm (Flex 'X' Muscle)
Sir/Mam I am going to be grabing you by the arm now.
Step 3: Vehicle Placement (Flex 'C' Muscle on Suspect)
Sir/Mam I am going to be placing you in the vehicle now, please watch your head.
Step 4: Miranda Rights Recitation (Read 1 to 3 times)
"You are under arrest, You have the right to remain silent, Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law. You have the right to an attorney, If you cannot afford an attorney, one will be appointed to you by the state if available. Do you understand the rights I just read to you?"
*If suspect ignores or states they don't understand, wait 5 seconds and repeat (up to 3 times total). After 3 attempts, inform them you read them 3 times.
Step 5: DOC Transport Radio Call
(BADGE NUMBER) To Dispatch, I am 10-17 to DOC with (NUMBER) of 10-15 and convoy.
Step 6: DOC Jumpsuit Size Check
Sir/Mam What size jumpsuit are you?
/me grabs a *SIZE* jumpsuit from behind the counter
Step 7: Search & Confiscation (Flex 'G' -> Org -> Search)
Sir/Mam I am going to search you for any identification and illegal items.
*Seize weapons with serial 'XXX' or government weapons. Allow 'AMMO' weapons only if valid gun license held. Log photos in #confiscation-logs.
Step 8: Final Jumpsuit & Jailing (Flex 'G' -> Org -> Arrest)
Sir/Mam I am going to be putting you in your jumpsuit now.
Step 9: 10-99 Booking Completed Radio Call
(BADGE NUMBER) to dispatch, show my last 10-15 is now 10-99 and I am 10-19.

block §2.4 List of Prohibited Items

Possession of any items listed below is subject to immediate confiscation and criminal charges under P.C. 2.4.1 (1-3 units: $15,000 fine) or P.C. 2.4.2 (3+ units: $20,000 fine + 30 months prison, Felony Class D).

  • Unregistered Firearms: All firearms without a serial number (tagged as XXX).
  • State Weapons: Government-issued weapons or melees (FIB / SAHP / LSPD / GOV / NG markings).
  • State Armor: Ballistic vests with state agency markings.
  • Illegal Body Armor: Ballistic vests of any color except standard legal gray (Gang vests, Louis Vuitton, patterned vests).
  • Weapon Components: Spare weapon parts and modification kits.
  • Facial Coverings: State-issued tactical balaclavas.
  • Electronic Tools: Vehicle scanners, Engine blockers, and Search Base Hack units.
  • Burglar Equipment: Lockpicks, master key sets, or illegal door bypass tools.
  • False Credentials: Counterfeit identification badges or forged police IDs.
  • Countermeasures: Anti-radar detectors and vehicle jammers.
  • Financial Contraband: Counterfeit currency.
  • Surveillance: People scanners and illicit tracking bugs.
  • Stolen Goods: Televisions, boxed furniture, appliances without purchase receipts.
  • Controlled Deliveries: Medical supplies and alcohol outside authorized commercial transport (P.C. 2.4.3).
